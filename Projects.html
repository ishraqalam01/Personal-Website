Projects

Research Exploration Opportunity — DGP Lab

Developed an image recognition model, a food recipe suggestion application, and a house price prediction model under DGP Lab Researcher Zixin (Nicole) Zhao. Worked with Python, PyTorch, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Pandas to organize and visualize data collected from the CIFAR-10 dataset.

Autonomous Ball Collection Rover

Programmed a BoeBot using PBASIC to autonomously pick up a ball and bring it to a scoring hole after crossing a rugged terrain. Modeled arms and mounts using CAD, and set precise code with predicted movement, scoring Top 3 out of 20+ competitors.

ESP32 Robotic Arm

Designed a 3-axis robotic arm controlled dually with a joystick and Wi-Fi using an ESP32 to manipulate three mini servos, LEDs, and MB102. Simulated arm designs and grabbing mechanisms using CAD.

Poké-Coaster — WonderCoaster Challenge 2024

As Team Leader, I coordinated the construction of an 80x80x60 cm roller coaster, maximizing creativity and marble efficiency. Installed components with power tools, and researched material properties capable of withstanding extreme weather. Minimized costs, and ensured the marble stayed on track at all times.

ESP32 Automatic Clothesline

Created an automatic clothesline on an ESP32 with Arduino C. It controlled if clothes should stay out or reel in, depending on exposure to water and increased temperatures with sensors.
